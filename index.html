Crebit does not directly hold your funds or process bank transfers. When you scan a UPI QR code, Crebit parses the merchant details and amount, creates a record in your ledger, and gives you a 1-tap shortcut to launch your installed UPI payment apps (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) to execute the payment through standard NPCI banking rails.

When you record any expense transaction, you can toggle "Split Expense". You can either split it equally among N people or enter your custom share. Crebit automatically calculates the remaining receivable balance, tracks it on your Splits dashboard, and lets you log settlements (repayments) into any of your bank accounts or cash in batches.

In Crebit, accounts are classified by type (Bank Account, Cash, Savings, Wallet, or Credit Card). While standard accounts are counted as positive assets, Credit Card balances are treated as liabilities (money owed). Your net worth is accurately computed as Total Assets minus Total Credit Card debt.
